# Roadmap: Business Technologist Skill Stack

> **A business technologist who can use AI and software to solve enterprise problems.**

Your advantage is not knowing 17 Python libraries. It comes from understanding:
**“Here is the business problem → here is the process → here is the data → here is the system → here is where AI helps → here is how we integrate it → here is the ROI.”**

---

## 1. The 5-Part Profile Breakdown

### 35% — AI, Data & Technical Systems
Python, SQL, APIs & integrations, Databases, Git, LLMs, Agents, RAG, Automation, Cloud, Data pipelines, Enterprise integration, Software architecture, AI evaluation & observability.

### 25% — Business & Finance
Corporate finance, Financial analysis, Strategy, Economics, Product management, Operations, B2B business models, Enterprise processes, Business cases & ROI, Digital transformation.

### 15% — Communication, Sales & Influence
Presentations, Business writing, Public speaking, Negotiation, Customer discovery, Sales, Stakeholder management, Executive communication.

### 15% — Product & Entrepreneurship
Customer problem discovery, MVP development, Prototyping, Product validation, Pricing, Distribution, Go-to-market, Building → selling → learning → rebuilding.

### 10% — Systems Thinking & Professional Depth
Process modeling, Requirements engineering, Architecture thinking, Risk management, Cybersecurity basics, Data governance, AI governance, Project management.

---

## 2. The Technical Stack (Build bottom-up)

**Layer 1 — Core engineering**
Python, SQL, Git, Linux, APIs, HTTP/REST, Databases, Testing, Debugging.

**Layer 2 — Data & AI**
Pandas, Data pipelines, ML fundamentals, LLMs, Embeddings, Vector databases, RAG, Agents, Evaluation, Prompt engineering.

**Layer 3 — Enterprise systems**
Authentication, OAuth, Webhooks, Message queues, Microservices basics, ERP/CRM integration, SAP basics, Cloud architecture, Docker, CI/CD.

**Layer 4 — AI applications**
AI automation, AI copilots, Document intelligence, RAG systems, AI agents, Workflow orchestration, Enterprise AI platforms.

*Note: RAG and agents sit on top of APIs, databases, authentication, etc. The full chain is API → authentication → database → business process → LLM → RAG → workflow → monitoring → deployment.*

---

## 3. The One Skill to Over-invest In: Architecture

This is the biggest missing piece for many. You want to eventually be able to look at a company and say:
> “Their CRM has this data, their ERP has that data, their documents are here, these APIs expose these capabilities, this workflow can be automated, and this AI component should sit here.”

Sketch something like:
```text
Customer
   ↓
Web / CRM
   ↓
API Layer
   ↓
Business Logic
   ↓
Database
   ↓
LLM / RAG
   ↓
Automation / Workflow
   ↓
ERP / CRM / Email
```
And explain every box. Practice this in `ARCHITECTURE.md` after every project.

---

## 4. The Mental Model: Intersection of Disciplines

Don't treat the 5 areas as separate subjects.

*   **Build**: AI + technology
*   **Understand**: Business + finance
*   **Influence**: Communication + sales
*   **Create**: Entrepreneurship + product

The real goal is the intersection:
**Business problem → Analyze it → Design solution → Build it → Explain it → Sell it → Deploy it → Measure ROI**

## 5. Target Roles

AI transformation, AI product management, AI solutions/consulting, Digital transformation, Enterprise AI, Data/AI strategy, and eventually AI-focused entrepreneurship (rather than conventional software engineering roles).
